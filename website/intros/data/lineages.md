# Nemotron and WizardLM, in detail

Two families of recipes shaped the pipeline more than the others. Nemotron is a staged factory with a reward model at
every gate; WizardLM makes instructions harder and proves they got harder. This page records what each actually did,
operator by operator, so the choices in `data_synthetic` can be traced to their source.
